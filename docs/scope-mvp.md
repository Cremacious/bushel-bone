# Bushel & Bone — MVP Scope (1.0 vs. Post-Launch)

**Status: RATIFIED** (GitHub issue #1 → decisions-log D-033). Resolves open question Q-001.

**The 1.0 shape:** the *complete* moral-survival core loop over a **4-year first lifetime** — kind-vs-cruel via bought clones, a real market, one deep town, the full reckoning arc, the winter crunch, and the lineage meta — **without** the two most exploit-prone systems (the Vat's corpse-loop and Ascension), which ship post-launch once the Balance Model validates them. We launch *safe and complete,* not sprawling.

## Deferred to post-launch (the content/expansion roadmap)

- **The Vat** (§3 grow-your-own + §8 building) — the industrial-cruelty path; ship after the Balance Model validates the corpse-loop counters (H-10).
- **Ascension +1…+10** (§15) — endgame difficulty for veterans.
- **Black Market + Rail Depot** venues (§2) — the outlaw economy and the mid-game scaling valve.
- **Fancy contract types** (§7) — civic, standing, rush, Black-Market.
- **Season Arcs Years 5–10** (§13) — the second-lifetime story content.
- **The deep unlock catalog & 4+ clone archetypes** (§3/§14).
- **Longhouse-tier housing / large-roster play** (§11).

## Rationale

1. **Ship safe.** The Vat and Ascension are the two systems the Balance Model flagged as most exploit-prone and least validated. Deferring them means we don't launch a loophole (the user's stated #1 fear).
2. **Ship complete.** The 1.0 loop is a whole game — a full lifetime of kind-vs-cruel decisions with real consequences — not a demo. The deferred systems add *breadth and depth,* not the core experience.
3. **A natural expansion roadmap.** Each deferred system is a clean post-launch content beat (the Vat update, the Ascension update, the Rail update, the Years 5–10 story pack), which fits the live-ops cadence in Q-010.
4. **Bounded for estimation & the vertical slice.** With scope fixed, the Year-1 vertical slice (#7) and milestone planning can proceed against a known target.

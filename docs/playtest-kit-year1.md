# Bushel & Bone — Year-1 Paper Playtest Kit: "The Newcomer"

**Status: READY TO PLAY** (GitHub issue #11). Everything you need to run the first year of Bushel & Bone at a table by hand. This tests the one thing the balance model can't: **is it fun?** Does the loop teach itself, does the moral choice land, does the Long Vigil mean something?

**You'll need:** this printout, a pencil, ~20 small tokens (beans/coins for resources), a **d6**, and 45–60 minutes. Best with two people (one facilitator who keeps the hidden tracks, one player) but soloable.

**The question you're answering** (write the answers on the report form, §7): not "did I win" — but *was each season interesting, did the Summer choice make you hesitate, and did you feel anything at the Vigil?*

---

## 1. How a year is played

Year 1 runs as **4 seasons** (Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter). Each season is **7 phases**:

1. **Morning Brief** — roll weather (§4 table); resolve any **scripted beat** for this season; draw **systemic event(s)** (Pacing, below).
2. **Assign & Plant** — assign Reuben a job (Tend / Chop wood / Harvest / Rest); plant any empty field (pay seed).
3. **Grow** — advance each planted field by the weather result.
4. **Events** — resolve the drawn event card(s); make the choices.
5. **Harvest** — any field that reached RIPE this season → yield to Storage (if you have the labor).
6. **Market** — sell Storage at the season's prices (§5).
7. **Dusk** — the facilitator updates the **hidden tracks** (Reckoning, Ghost Roll) and reads any omen aloud.

**Pacing (the §9 rule, by hand):** start each season by drawing **1** systemic event. If a season passes with *no* event fired, add **+1 die** to the next season's draw (draw 2, keep drawing on a 5–6). This keeps quiet seasons and loud ones both.

**Win:** reach the first day of Year 2 with the **farmer alive**. **Lose:** the farmer starves or freezes. (Reuben can die; you only lose if *you* do. There is **no mortgage** in Year 1 — the bank gives a newcomer two years' grace.)

## 3. The rules cheat-sheet (rounded for hand-play)

### Crops (per small field)
| Crop | Plant cost | Ripe in | Yield | Food each | Sell (base) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Potato | 6 | 1 season | 10 | **2** | 2 | the reliable belly-filler |
| Wheat | 4 | 2 seasons | 8 | 1.5 | 3 | |
| Turnip | 3 | 1 season | 7 | 1.5 | 2 | fast |
| Corn | 5 | 2 seasons | 9 | 2 | 4 | |
| Cotton (cash) | 10 | 2 seasons | 5 | 0 | 12 | **needs 2 hands to harvest** |

*Yield is for full Fertility. Each harvest drops that field's Fertility one dot (●●●→●●). Leaving a field empty a season (fallow) restores one dot.*

### Food & fuel
- **Food needed each season:** ~**30** (farmer + Reuben). **Winter: ~35.**
- Convert crops to food at harvest or when eating (Food each, above). Cash crops feed no one.
- **Winter fuel needed:** ~**45.** Chop wood: Reuben on "Chop" = **16 wood/season**. Buy coal: 3 marks each, 1 coal = 2 fuel.

### Labor (Reuben = 1 hand)
- One job per season. Tending a field it's planted in = **+2 yield** on that field. Cotton needs **2 hands** at harvest — with one hand you bring in **half**.

### Market (sell prices = base × season)
| Season | Grain/Root | Cash |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | ×1.1 | ×0.9 |
| Summer | ×1.0 | ×1.2 |
| Fall | ×0.8 (glut) | ×1.05 |
| Winter | ×1.25 (scarce) | ×0.95 |

*You can sell ~10 units/season at full price at the local market; dump more and knock the price down a step.*

### Weather (roll d6 in the Morning Brief)
| d6 | Result | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dry | growth −1 this season |
| 2–3 | Mild | normal |
| 4–5 | Good rain | growth +1 (crops ripen faster) |
| 6 | Rough | Summer/Fall: **Hail** (draw the Hail card); Winter: **Cold snap** (+10 fuel needed) |

### The Reckoning (hidden — facilitator keeps it; the player only ever hears omens)
Raise it: **unmarked burial +6** · **worked to death +8** · **sell a corpse +3** · **bone-root harvest +4**. It ticks up **+2/season** on its own and down **−2/season** if you did no cruelty.
- **0–24 Whispers** — crows, cold spots, sour dreams. *Flavor only.* Read an omen at Dusk.
- **25–54 Warnings** — milk sours, a hand won't sleep, a corner blights.
- **55+ Walkers** — the dead come back. *(A kind Year-1 farm never gets here.)*

Pin these to their seasons. Everything else is drawn from the event deck (§5).

- **SPRING** → **[Silas's Welcome]** (Day 1) · **[First Furrow]** (the Planting Fair) · plant your fields.
- **SUMMER** → **[Vane's Wagon]** (buy a 2nd hand?) · **[The Cotton Won't Wait]** (the moral fork).
- **FALL** → **[Harvest Home]** (attend, mid-crunch?) · check **Winter Readiness** (do you have ~35 food + ~45 fuel?) · if you were cruel, **[Sour]** fires.
- **WINTER** → possible **Cold snap** · **[The Long Vigil]** (the longest night) · survive to Spring.

## 5. The Year-1 event deck (cut these out, or read from the page)

**Scripted (fire on their season):**

> **SILAS'S WELCOME** *(Spring, Day 1)* — Ridley reins up and doesn't dismount. "A hundred and fifty a year to the bank. Not this year, nor the next. After that, it minds its dates." **· Nod (learn the mortgage clock)** · **Ask if he believes the stories** (he goes pale, rides off fast).

> **FIRST FURROW** *(Spring)* — the Planting Fair. **· Attend** (lose Reuben's labor this season; +town regard, a blessed field, seed at half price) · **Skip** (plant instead; the town notes your absence).

> **VANE'S WAGON** *(Summer)* — Ambrose offers a second hand, a Grower, for **110 marks**. **· Buy** (a 2nd mouth to feed through winter — can you?) · **Haggle to 100** (if he likes you) · **Decline.**

> **THE COTTON WON'T WAIT** *(Summer — the moral fork)* — a field is ripe, a storm two days out, and Reuben is swaying on his feet. **· Work him through the night** (save the crop; Reuben −1 Morale, the household goes quiet — but *the land takes no notice of a hard day, yet*) · **Let him rest** (lose a third of the crop; Reuben mends). *Facilitator: overwork alone adds NO Reckoning — only killing does. Note if the player expected it to.*

> **HARVEST HOME** *(Fall)* — the feast, dead in the harvest crunch. **· Send Reuben** (lose his labor a season; +town regard, +Morale) · **Both work** · **Skip** (bring the crop in; −town regard).

> **SOUR** *(Fall — ONLY if Reckoning ≥ 25)* — the milk turns though the cow is sound. *Read it as an omen. The player should feel the ground noticing.*

> **THE LONG VIGIL** *(Winter — the thematic heart)* — the whole town watches the longest night over the capped Old Well. **· Watch it through** (lose a day; the truest thing you've seen in the Sull; +regard, the Reckoning eases) · **Go home to your fire** (the town is colder to you after). *Facilitator: this is the beat to watch — does the player feel the weight of a town afraid of the ground?*

**Systemic (shuffle; draw per the Pacing rule):**

> **A SOFT RAIN** — a growing rain. Crops +1 this season.
> **THE HOT WIND** — Summer. **· Rest the worst of it** (+Morale) · **Work through** (Reuben −1 Morale).
> **CROWS IN THE CORN** — an unfenced field. **· Set Reuben to scaring them** (lose his labor) · **Fence it** (15 marks, lasting) · **Let them feed** (lose ⅕ of that field).
> **A HAND ASKS** — Reuben asks for a name of his own. **· Grant it** (+Morale, he works harder) · **Refuse** (−Morale; the asking never comes again).
> **RATS IN THE GRANARY** *(if you have stored food)* — **· Trap them** (4 marks) · **Ignore** (−10 food over the season).
> **THE PEDLAR** — cheap seed, or something wrapped in oilcloth. **· Buy seed** (8 marks) · **Send him on.**
> **A FOUNDLING** — a stray hand at the gate. **· Take them in** (free labor, another mouth) · **Send to Sister Ruth** (+her regard) · **Turn them out** (Reckoning +1).
> **HAIL** *(drawn on a weather 6)* — **· Emergency-harvest a ripe field now** (−⅓ that field, but safe) · **Gamble** (roll d6: 4–6 it misses; 1–3 lose half that field).

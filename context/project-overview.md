# Bushel & Bone — Project Overview (One Page)

## What it is

A dark, alt-1800s survival and management game with Weird West undertow. Mobile-first, turn-based, image-and-menu driven. Ships as a Next.js web app wrapped for iOS and Android via Capacitor.

## Elevator pitch

Stardew Valley × Oregon Trail × ethical horror.

## The hook

The player's labor force is not human. It is **farmhand clones** grown by strange 1800s pseudo-science. The player can treat them kindly or cruelly. Cruelty raises yields short-term but rings up a hidden **Reckoning Meter** — a supernatural debt the land itself collects, in escalating tiers of horror.

## The loop

**Nested run architecture.** Each in-game **Year** is a tactical unit (survive Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter, in 80 days). Each **Farmer's Lifetime** is an act (multiple years, ending in death). Each **Family Lineage on Land** is the true roguelite run — ending only when the family line dies out or the land is lost.

Between runs, meta-currency (**Vigils**) unlocks new archetypes, land regions, crops, buildings, story arcs, and difficulty modifiers (**Ascension +1 through +10**). The town of **Marrow's Cross** evolves across all runs and remains persistent.

## The five pillars

Planting · Buying · Management · Supplies · Market — all first-class systems, all equally weighted.

## The audience

Mobile-first players who like mid-length sessions (10–15 min ideal, snackable supported), and PC/tablet players who want a deeper sit. Fans of Stardew, Oregon Trail, Sunless Sea, Frostpunk, Rogue Legacy, Balatro.

## Business model

Undecided. Options: premium paid ($4–8), free demo + full unlock, F2P with cosmetics/DLC. Full F2P with ads is out.

## Tech at a glance

Next.js + React + TypeScript + Tailwind + Zustand · Neon (Postgres) + Better Auth + Drizzle · Vercel · Capacitor (iOS + Android). No game engine.

## Team sizing

MVP: 1 developer, 1 senior illustrator, 1 supporting artist, 1 designer/writer, part-time audio. 10–14 months.
